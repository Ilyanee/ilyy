My favorite mangas:

Click an image to get the details !

When you talk about anime, we have to remember that, originals aside, we talk about adaptations. Not going too deep on the matter, let's just talk about the mangas that can give birth to worldwide reknowned anime or remain untouched masterpieces.
While the visual aspect of anime is mainly dependent on the animation studio, mangas are (let's agree on this) solely dependant of their authors. Hence, you can find people loving some mangas for the different reasons even though it may be the same work!
I could go for a lengthy talk about genres, authors, series, characteristics and stuff, but for now I'll just name a few of my most liked artists below this list of my favorite mangas.

  • Sui Ishida (石田スイ)

    • Famous author of the even more famous Tokyo Ghoul, one of my favorite artists since I first read this series. (I read it five times by the time I write those lines). Breathtaking artwork, amazing compositions, excellent storytelling... I can't encourage you enough to look into his work.

  • Eiichiro Oda (尾田 栄一郎)

    • Do I need to introduce the man himself? Although numbers do no stand for quality, there is a reason One Piece does such huge numbers, I can assure you. You think you knew wordbuilding? Read One Piece and you will see that he brought the word to a whole other dimension.

  • Inoue Takehiko (井上 雄彦)
